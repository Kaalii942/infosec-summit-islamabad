About the Summit

InfoSec Summit Islamabad 2026 is Pakistan's premier cybersecurity gahering, dedicated to fostering innovation, awareness, and collaboration in the digital security landscape. Scheduled for February 10, 2026, at the Islamabad Chamber of Commerce & Industry (ICCI), this summit serves as a beacon for aspiring cybersecurity experts and seasoned professionals alike.

Our mission is to bridge the significant gap between academic learning and industry requirements. By bringing together over 350+ enthusiastic students, researchers, and technical leaders, we create an environment where knowledge is shared freely through technical talks, hands-on workshops, and immersive cyber-defense activities.

In an era where digital threats are evolving faster than ever, "Pakistan's Premium Cyber Summit" aims to equip the next generation of defenders with the tools, connections, and mindset needed to secure our nation's digital future.
